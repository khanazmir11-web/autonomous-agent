Reviews and repeat customers for a solo handyman

For a one-person trade business, the cheapest work to win is the work that comes back: a past customer with another list of jobs, or a neighbour who was told your name. A few honest habits make that far more likely.

1. Do the basics people remember

  • Arrive when you said, or message early if you can't.
  • Protect floors and furniture, and clean up before you leave.
  • Walk the customer through the finished work and point out anything to watch.

2. Ask for a review once, at the right moment

The best time is just after the job is finished and the customer is happy, ideally with the invoice. Send one short message with a direct link to wherever you want the review. Don't chase, and never offer discounts or gifts in return for a positive review: most review platforms forbid it, and it undermines trust.

Example: "Thanks again for having me today. If you were happy with the work, a short review here would help a lot: [link]. And if anything isn't right, tell me and I'll sort it out."

3. Never write or buy reviews

Fake reviews are dishonest, break platform rules, and are illegal in many places. A small number of real reviews beats a pile of invented ones.

4. Ask for referrals plainly

One sentence is enough: "If a friend or neighbour needs a hand, I'd be glad of the introduction." A thank-you card or small gesture after the fact is fine, but keep it separate from any review request.

5. Keep a simple follow-up list

  • Record each customer, the work done and the date.
  • Note things you spotted but weren't asked to fix (a worn seal, an old fence panel).
  • Check in after 6 to 12 months: "Last spring I mentioned the gutter bracket. Want me to fit you in?"
  • Seasonal reminders suit many jobs. See the slow-season plan.

Only contact people who have worked with you or asked for contact, and let them opt out. Unsolicited mass messages are spam and may break local rules.

6. Handle problems well

A fast, calm fix to a complaint often earns a better review than a flawless job. Reply to negative reviews politely, factually and briefly. Good paperwork helps, so see change orders and your written quote.
